# Authoring tools

> Authoring Tool: Any application(s) that can be used by authors (alone or collaboratively) to create or modify content for use by authors or end users.
>
> — [Definition for ATAG](https://github.com/w3c-cg/atag/wiki/Definition-for-ATAG), ATAG Community Group wiki.

### Methodology notes

- CMS & site builders and rich text editors together come to 50 mentions, level with Markup & text in fifth place.
- Office & documents was discussed in the most meetings (16 of 27 meetings counted).
- Code & AI agents is level with Office & documents on mentions: the 2026-11-13 discussion of code editors added 5.
- Markup & text comes almost entirely from one long discussion of Markdown.

Sources: 27 meeting minutes, from 21 November 2025 to 13 November 2026 (the repository's `minutes/` folder has 22 of them), the charter, the Use Cases and Requirements (UCR) draft, and GitHub discussions [#7], [#12] and [#15].

- **Meetings**: number of meetings (out of 27) where the tool came up as an example. Meetings after 13 November 2026 are not counted.
- **Mentions**: approximate count of mentions in the minutes. Zoom transcripts are noisy.
- Rows with 0 meetings come only from GitHub discussion #12 or the UCR draft.
- Not counted: the group's own use of GitHub, Google Docs, Zoom and ReSpec to run its work.
- **Prominence**: high = 6+ meetings or 20+ mentions overall; medium = 3–5 meetings or 5+ mentions; low = otherwise.
