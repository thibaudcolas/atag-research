# Decisions and open questions

Two registers from the published minutes: what the group has settled, and what it has not. Dates are the meeting where the position was recorded; current as of 13 November 2026.

## Decided

| Decision                                                                                                      | Date                                                                                             |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| ReSpec conversion: lettered Parts A and B become numbered parts.                                              | [16 January 2026](https://www.w3.org/2026/01/16-atag-minutes.html)                               |
| ATAG stays a separate specification, not folded into WCAG 3. AGWG chairs agree.                               | [13 February 2026](https://www.w3.org/2026/02/13-atag-minutes.html)                              |
| Joint path with Accessibility Standards Canada under a memorandum of understanding.                           | [13 February 2026](https://www.w3.org/2026/02/13-atag-minutes.html)                              |
| Three non-normative reports (Use Cases and Requirements, Gap Analysis, Research); no test suites or software. | [CG charter](https://w3c-cg.github.io/atag/ATAG_Charter.html), in effect 20 April 2026           |
| Tool developers are the primary audience; evaluators and procurement are secondary.                           | [21 August 2026](https://w3c-cg.github.io/atag/minutes/minutes_Aug21_2026)                       |
| No obligation of backwards compatibility with ATAG 2.0.                                                       | [28 August 2026](https://w3c-cg.github.io/atag/minutes/minutes_Aug28_2026)                       |
| Authoring tool functions are a cross-cutting layer, each on a basic-to-advanced spectrum.                     | [11 September 2026](https://w3c-cg.github.io/atag/minutes/minutes_Sep11_2026)                    |
| User roles: creator, maintainer, consumer.                                                                    | [25 September 2026](https://w3c-cg.github.io/atag/minutes/minutes_Sep25_2026)                    |
| Use cases are structured by authoring actions rather than by stakeholder.                                     | [2 October 2026](https://w3c-cg.github.io/atag/minutes/minutes_Oct02_2026)                       |
| Personal-use content is in scope.                                                                             | [9 October 2026](https://w3c-cg.github.io/atag/minutes/minutes_Oct09_2026), not yet in the draft |
| A Working Group is added rather than converting the CG; the CG persists as its incubator.                     | [13 November 2026](https://w3c-cg.github.io/atag/minutes/minutes_Nov13_2026)                     |
| Miriam Fukushima is CG co-chair, and WG co-chair once the charter is approved.                                | [13 November 2026](https://w3c-cg.github.io/atag/minutes/minutes_Nov13_2026)                     |

## Not started

- **Gap Analysis report**, listed in the charter.
- **Research report.** Unowned ideas: co-design sessions ([10 April 2026](https://w3c-cg.github.io/atag/minutes/minutes_Apr10_2026)), a literature review on AI and authoring, a CMS landscape scan, mining accessibility issue queues, and an AI benchmark. See [contribution ideas](contribution-ideas/README.md).
- **Benchmark as a conformance signal**, raised [27 February 2026](https://www.w3.org/2026/02/27-atag-minutes.html), [27 March 2026](https://www.w3.org/2026/03/27-atag-minutes.html), and [8 May 2026](https://w3c-cg.github.io/atag/minutes/minutes_May08_2026); Abhinav Kannan volunteered to research prompting strategies ([14 August 2026](https://w3c-cg.github.io/atag/minutes/minutes_Aug14_2026)).

The [Use cases and requirements draft](../knowledge-base/atag-cg/ucr.md#what-the-draft-does-not-cover) has its own gap list.
