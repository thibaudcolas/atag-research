# CG contributions

Research this workspace contributes to the [ATAG Community Group's](../knowledge-base/atag-cg/README.md) work: its [Use Cases and Requirements](https://w3c-cg.github.io/atag/docs/ucr/) draft, the ATAG 2.0 review, and the incubation of guidance for AI authoring tools.

The CG meets regularly and works in public. This workspace keeps copies of the group's minutes, issues, discussions, and draft documents in `local/`, which is not published with this site. Pages here document findings drawn from those sources, with citations. Unpublished sources stay cited rather than copied: the [authoring tools](../knowledge-base/authoring-tools.md) list and the [WCAG 3 provision mapping](wcag3-provisions-review/wcag-3-provisions-review.md) summarise what the discussions and minutes establish.

The group's own documents are described in [ATAG CG](../knowledge-base/atag-cg/README.md), with separate notes on the [Use cases and requirements draft](../knowledge-base/atag-cg/ucr.md) and the [ATAG 2.0 review findings](../knowledge-base/atag-cg/atag-2-review.md).

## Pages

- [Decisions and open questions](open-questions.md): what the group has settled, what it has not, and which deliverables have no owner.
- [WCAG 3 provisions review](wcag3-provisions-review/wcag-3-provisions-review.md): the group's classification exercise, its proposed relationship values, and the open questions.
- [WCAG 3 provisions relevance](wcag3-provisions-review/wcag-3-provisions-relevance.md): every provision in the draft, in guideline order, for that review.
- [Contribution ideas](contribution-ideas/README.md): research this workspace could contribute.
- [Personal notes](personal-notes.md): informal notes from meetings and brainstorming.
