# CG contribution ideas

Research at the periphery of ATAG that could contribute to the group's deliverables.

## Fresh

### Automated audits

Automated accessibility and web sustainability audits for AI-assisted authoring. References in [Automated audits](automated-audits.md).

### Benchmarking

Benchmarking accessibility in audits / testing / coding. References in [Benchmarking](benchmarking.md).

### Agentic use cases

Agentic creator or consumer use cases for the [Use Cases and Requirements](../../knowledge-base/atag-cg/ucr.md) draft, which has none. Six candidate scenarios, the requirements they imply, and the draft's structural constraints are in [Agentic use cases](agentic-use-cases.md).

### Human agency and levels of author control

A short position defining levels of author control (inform, suggest, ask, act with confirmation, act and notify) would give the terminology and the requirement wording somewhere to land.
