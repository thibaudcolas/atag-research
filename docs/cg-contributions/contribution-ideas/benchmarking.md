# Benchmarking

> Right now I'm on benchmarking accessibility and web sustainability audits specifically, not authoring tools as a whole, but that feels very feasible too.

The group has asked for this since its first months with no owner: Wendy Reid proposed a benchmark instead of a test suite ([27 February 2026](https://www.w3.org/2026/02/27-atag-minutes.html), [27 March 2026](https://www.w3.org/2026/03/27-atag-minutes.html), [8 May 2026](https://w3c-cg.github.io/atag/minutes/minutes_May08_2026)). Evelyn Wightman's question, whether output that is accessible 95% of the time is acceptable, is the same problem stated as a threshold ([17 July 2026](https://w3c-cg.github.io/atag/minutes/minutes_Jul17_2026)). Abhinav Kannan volunteered to research prompting strategies, and Sambhavi Chandrashekar asked whether the work would be crowdsourced or assigned ([14 August 2026](https://w3c-cg.github.io/atag/minutes/minutes_Aug14_2026)).

## References

- [Accessible AI Code](https://github.com/accessible-ai-code-benchmark/accessible-ai-code): public test harness benchmarking how well LLMs generate accessible HTML code.
- [A11y LLM Eval report](https://microsoft.github.io/a11y-llm-eval-report/): accessibility evaluation results for different models, instruction sets, and skills.
- [microsoft/a11y-llm-eval](https://github.com/microsoft/a11y-llm-eval): the eval tool behind the report.
- [AIMAC, the AI Model Accessibility Checker](https://aimac.ai/): leaderboard ranking AI models on the accessibility of the web pages they generate, published by the GAAD Foundation with ServiceNow.
- [WebAccessBench](https://conesible.de/wab/whitepaper_webaccessbench.pdf): white paper shared when the benchmark idea was first raised ([27 February 2026](https://www.w3.org/2026/02/27-atag-minutes.html)).
