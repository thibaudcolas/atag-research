# ATAG research

A shared workspace for research on the [Authoring Tool Accessibility Guidelines (ATAG)](https://www.w3.org/TR/ATAG20/) and the ATAG Community Group.

## Contents

We organize information across a small number of layers:

1. [Contributing](./contributing/README.md): guidance on how to work within this workspace. Read this first, for all types of tasks.
2. [Knowledge base](./knowledge-base/README.md): general-purpose information relevant for a wide range of tasks, with evergreen upstream references on [ATAG](./knowledge-base/atag/README.md), the [ATAG WG](./knowledge-base/atag-wg/README.md), the [ATAG CG](./knowledge-base/atag-cg/README.md), and [WCAG](./knowledge-base/wcag.md).
3. [CG contributions](./cg-contributions/README.md): research this workspace contributes to the ATAG Community Group.
