# ATAG CG

Research on the [Authoring Tool Accessibility Guidelines (ATAG)](https://www.w3.org/TR/ATAG20/) Community Group: its guidelines, current Working Group discussions, and the provisions in the draft ATAG 2.1 update.

The ATAG Community Group meets regularly and works in public. Meeting minutes, issues, and discussions are collected in `local/atag-minutes/` and are not published with this site. Pages here document findings drawn from those sources, with citations.
